What We Do

Three ways to know where your website stands. All three are offered at founding-client pricing while we take on our first clients — one price, everything included, no hidden fees.

1. Instant Check

A free first look at the signals anyone on the internet can already see: your security headers, your encryption (TLS), and whether your email is protected against impersonation. Quick, honest, and a good place to start.

2. Deep Scan + Plain-English Report

Websites change, and new problems show up quietly. Monthly Watch re-checks your site on a schedule and flags new issues early, so a small fix never gets the chance to become a bad week.

How It Works

1

Sign a one-page authorization

A short, plain document names exactly which systems are yours and in bounds. Nothing runs before it is signed.

2

We scan inside an agreed window

The rules of engagement are in writing before anything runs — what we check, when we check it, and when we stop and call you.

3

You get the plain-English report

Every finding explained: what we found, what it means for your business, and how to fix it, in order of importance.

4

You decide what to fix

We never change anything on your systems. The report is the map — what gets fixed, and by whom, is always your call.

What We Never Do

  • No attacks. We look and we report. We do not break in, and we do not try to.
  • No touching your data. Our checks read what your systems show the public. Your files, accounts, and customer information are never accessed.
  • No testing anyone else's systems. Only the systems named in your signed authorization — never a third party, never a neighbor on the same host.
  • No changes. We do not fix, patch, configure, or log into anything. You stay in control of your own house.
  • No surprises. Scope, timing, stop conditions, and reporting are agreed in writing before a single check runs.
